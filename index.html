About Me

Hey there! I’m a Cloud & AI Lead based in Buenos Aires, Argentina, driven by a deep love for building technology that scales. Day-to-day, I specialize in designing and architecting high-availability distributed systems, but lately, my main focus has been diving deep into modern AI, Agentic Engineering, and LLM Engineering. A lot of this hands-on focus comes straight out of my ongoing Master’s in Data Science at Universidad Austral, where I’ve been researching and experimenting with agent-based systems and language models.

I hold a degree in Computer Science from Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, and over the years, my primary toolkit has evolved around Python, Node.js, Java, C#, and major cloud platforms—plus plenty of exposure to Big Data, IoT, and Blockchain. Right now, I lead the Cloud & Custom Architecture + Data/AI Practice at Capgemini. Before this, I spent years refining my skills at places like Okta, Viterra, NTT Data, GlobalLogic, and Chazki.

Outside of work, I’m a big-time traveler and a tech enthusiast who loves experimenting with new tools and emerging technologies just for the fun of it. I’m also a bit of a polyglot (currently fluent in 6 languages), a total map geek, and an avid reader of science, sci-fi, and history. Whenever I'm not tweaking code or learning a new tech stack, you'll probably find me planning my next trip or diving down a history rabbit hole!
